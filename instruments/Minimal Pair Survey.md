# Minimal-Pair Survey

This instrument was used to collect expert judgments about **cognitive avoidance** in short student interaction sequences.

## Instructions

Each item shows a sequence of problem-solving attempts by one student within the same skill. Based only on the displayed record, rate your agreement with the statement:

> The student showed a tendency to avoid difficult problems or seeking help.

There is no single correct answer; the task asks for professional judgment.

For each item, provide:

- **Agreement:** 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree)
- **Reason:** a brief explanation for the rating
